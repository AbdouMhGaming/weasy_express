description: User preference for Weasy Express dark mode after rejecting warm-tinted alternatives.

Use neutral charcoal and gray surfaces with legible light text for the optional dark mode. Keep red limited to the site's existing brand actions and accents, rather than tinting dark backgrounds brown, yellow, or rose.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected a warm ink and muted-rose dark palette as looking yellow or red, and asked for a normal dark mode.

**How to apply:** When adding dark styles to new sections, choose unsaturated neutral surfaces and check their text contrast; preserve intentional red hero and button branding without extending it to neutral panels.
